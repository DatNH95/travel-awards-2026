# Visual refinement — Current KV V2 rules

The current visual authority and asset policy are documented in [design-foundation.md](design-foundation.md). Previous V1 decomposition notes are archived and superseded.

- Surfaces remain `primary`, `brand-soft` and `brand-deep`; text, CTA, links and focus adapt through semantic variables.
- Primary brand moments use the official PNG V2 and campaign lockup. Major chapters may use the signature divider; minor sections use a star, line or typography. Content areas prefer whitespace.
- Use only retained logo/lockup/star/divider SVGs that render correctly. Legacy landscape exports are archived and excluded from composition and image overlays.
- Image treatments currently available: straight-edge editorial crop and full bleed. The source remains PNG V2; samples are illustrations, not supplied photography.
- Typography/line/graphic/image reveals remain restrained and finite. V2 layer depth is deferred until matching independent assets exist. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables motion and smooth scrolling.
- Current display font is explicitly temporary; no licensed brand font or font guideline is supplied.

Foundation layout, typography hierarchy and color palette are preserved. No Homepage is built.
